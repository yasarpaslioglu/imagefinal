COMP430 – Digital Image Processing
Final Term Project — Objective Evaluation Rubric (Round 1)
Purpose: Score each submission consistently against the Round-1 delivery rules. Round-1
scores are formative: they identify concrete gaps so that 3–4 short revision items can be
issued for Round 2 (deadline June 17, 2026).
Total: 100 points across 8 criteria, plus a set of pass/fail compliance gates and integrity
flags that are scored separately and can cap or penalize the final score.
A. Scoring scale (applied to every criterion)
Each criterion is scored on a 0–4 level, then multiplied by the criterion weight. Levels are
anchored as follows:
Level Label Meaning
4 Excellent Fully meets the requirement;
publication-quality; nothing
material missing.
3 Good Meets the requirement with
minor, non-critical gaps.
2 Adequate Partially meets it; noticeable
gaps that should be fixed.
1 Weak Present but seriously
deficient or superficial.
0 Missing Absent or unusable.
Criterion score = (level / 4) × weight. The levels above are anchors. When a submission
sits clearly between two anchors, an intermediate value (e.g., a half-level, or a point value
between the two anchor scores) may be assigned on that criterion's 0-to-weight scale, with a
one-line justification in the review. The total is the sum of the eight criterion scores. The sum
of weights is 100.

- Adequate test set / cross-validation; statistical care where relevant.
- Honest reporting (failure cases or limitations of the evaluation acknowledged).

C. Compliance gates (pass/fail — recorded, not weighted)
These are checklist items from the submission rules. They do not add points but are
reported per student, and unmet gates become first-priority Round-2 revision items. Severe
cases (e.g., wrong format entirely) may cap the Format criterion at level 1.
- G1 — Page count: 6–10 pages. (Flag: under 6 / over 10.)
- G2 — IEEE template: Word or LaTeX IEEE conference template actually used.
- G3 — Section completeness: All required sections a–g present, including
Acknowledgements with AI-use disclosure (f).
- G4 — GitHub repository: Public repo URL cited in the paper, with a proper
README.md.
- G5 — Submission packaging: Word → single .docx; LaTeX → an archive (.zip,
or .rar — both accepted) containing .tex, .bib, images/figures. (Note missing
sources; container type alone is not penalized.)
- G6 — Authorship/title block present.
D. Integrity flags (penalties)
- I-1 — Late submission: note if filename/metadata indicates LATE (record only; no
grading penalty applied — per instructor decision).
